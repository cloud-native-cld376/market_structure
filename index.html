Introduction

What Is Market Structure?

Simple Definition

Market Structure is the behavior, condition, and current flow of the market. It shows the direction price is moving through a series of highs and lows.

  • Price does never move in a straight line.
  • Price moves in waves: impulses (strong moves) followed by pullbacks (corrections).
  • Reading structure tells you who is in control: Buyers or Sellers.
Core Rule: "Never trade against the trend. Always align with the market structure."

Foundations

The 3 Market Trends

01
Uptrend (Bullish)

Price is moving UPWARDS. Buyers are stronger than sellers.

Pattern: Higher Highs (HH) & Higher Lows (HL)
02
Downtrend (Bearish)

Price is moving DOWNWARDS. Sellers are stronger than buyers.

Pattern: Lower Highs (LH) & Lower Lows (LL)
03
Sideways (Range)

Price moves HORIZONTAL between support and resistance.

Pattern: Equal Highs (EQH) & Equal Lows (EQL)
Quick Rule: In an Uptrend, we look for Buys. In a Downtrend, we look for Sells. In a Range, we wait for a breakout.

Market Trends 05
Definitions

The 4 Key Swing Points

Bullish Swings
HH Higher High

A peak that is higher than the previous peak.

HL Higher Low

A pullback low that stays higher than the previous low.

Bearish Swings
LH Lower High

A bounce peak that is lower than the previous peak.

LL Lower Low

A valley that drops lower than the previous valley.

Summary: Uptrend = HH + HL  |  Downtrend = LH + LL. Master these 4 abbreviations!

Trend Identification

Uptrend: Higher Highs & Higher Lows

How to Spot an Uptrend

Every new push creates a Higher High (HH), and every pullback stops above the previous low to form a Higher Low (HL).

  • Price creates: Low → High → HL → HH → HL → HH.
  • As long as price stays above the recent Higher Low, the uptrend is HEALTHY.
  • Traders look to buy at the Higher Low (pullback), not at the top of the Higher High.

Downtrend: Lower Highs & Lower Lows

How to Spot a Downtrend

Every drop creates a Lower Low (LL), and every bounce fails to break above the previous high, forming a Lower High (LH).

  • Price creates: High → Low → LH → LL → LH → LL.
  • As long as price stays below the recent Lower High, the downtrend is HEALTHY.
  • Traders look to sell at the Lower High (bounce), not at the very bottom of the Lower Low.

Sideways: Ranging Market

What is a Sideways Market?

When price is neither creating higher highs nor lower lows. It bounces back and forth between a Resistance ceiling and a Support floor.

  • Equal Highs (EQH): Price hits the same upper level multiple times and rejects.
  • Equal Lows (EQL): Price hits the same bottom level multiple times and bounces.
  • Tip for Beginners: Avoid trading in the middle of a range. Wait for price to break out clearly!

Continuation

Break of Structure (BOS)

What is a BOS?

BOS (Break of Structure) means the trend is CONTINUING in the same direction.

  • In an Uptrend: A BOS happens when price breaks above the previous swing high to make a new Higher High (HH).
  • In a Downtrend: A BOS happens when price breaks below the previous swing low to make a new Lower Low (LL).
  • Meaning: The trend is strong and healthy. Keep following it!
Key Rule: We want to see the candle body close beyond the level, not just a quick wick poke!

Reversal

Market Structure Shift (MSS / CHoCH)

What is a Market Structure Shift (MSS)?

An MSS (also called CHoCH) means the trend is REVERSING and changing direction.

  • From Uptrend to Downtrend: Price fails to continue up and breaks BELOW the last Higher Low (HL).
  • From Downtrend to Uptrend: Price breaks ABOVE the last Lower High (LH).
  • Meaning: The old trend is over. Stop trading the old direction and watch for the new trend!
Difference: BOS = Trend continues. MSS = Trend flips!

• MSS: Trend reversal (broken key low or high).
