Market Structure 01
Introduction

What Is Market Structure?

The Market's Story

Market Structure is the behavior and direction of price over time. It reveals whether Buyers (Bulls) or Sellers (Bears) are in control.

  • Price never moves in a straight line — it moves in natural waves.
  • Impulse Moves: Powerful pushes in the direction of the dominant trend.
  • Pullback Moves: Temporary breathers and retracements before continuation.
Core Rule: Never fight the market flow. Always trade in the direction of the structure.

Market Trends 05
Definitions

The 4 Key Swing Points

Bullish Swing Points
HH Higher High

A price peak that rises higher than the preceding peak. Shows strong buying momentum.

HL Higher Low

A pullback valley that stays higher than the preceding valley. Shows buyers defending price.

Bearish Swing Points
LH Lower High

A relief bounce peak that fails to reach the preceding peak. Shows weak buyers.

LL Lower Low

A drop that plummets lower than the preceding valley. Shows aggressive selling pressure.

Cheat Sheet: Uptrend = HH + HL  |  Downtrend = LH + LL. Always mark these 4 points on your chart!

Trend Identification

Uptrend: Higher Highs & Higher Lows

How to Spot an Uptrend

An uptrend is characterized by a series of consecutive Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).

  • Each new push climbs above the previous peak (forming a new HH).
  • Each pullback pauses above the previous low (forming a safe HL).
  • Pro Trader Tip: Look to buy at the Higher Low (pullback), never FOMO buy at the peak of a Higher High.
Healthy Trend Rule: As long as price stays above the most recent Higher Low, the uptrend is valid and healthy.

Downtrend: Lower Highs & Lower Lows

How to Spot a Downtrend

A downtrend is characterized by a series of consecutive Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).

  • Each new wave plummets below the previous low (forming a new LL).
  • Each bounce is weak and stalls below the previous high (forming a LH).
  • Pro Trader Tip: Look to sell at the Lower High (bounce), never chase sells at the very bottom of a Lower Low.
Healthy Trend Rule: As long as price remains below the most recent Lower High, the downtrend is intact and active.

Sideways: Ranging Market

What is a Range?

When price is neither creating higher highs nor lower lows. Instead, price is trapped bouncing between two horizontal boundaries.

  • Resistance Ceiling: Equal Highs (EQH) where sellers reject price pushes.
  • Support Floor: Equal Lows (EQL) where buyers defend and bounce price.
  • Golden Beginner Rule: Avoid trading in the noisy middle of the range. Wait for price to break out clearly!
Strategy: Wait for a clean breakout candle that closes outside the range boundaries.

Continuation

Break of Structure (BOS)

What is a BOS?

BOS (Break of Structure) is the clearest confirmation that the existing trend is CONTINUING in the same direction.

  • Bullish BOS: Price breaks above the previous swing high to print a fresh Higher High (HH).
  • Bearish BOS: Price breaks below the previous swing low to print a fresh Lower Low (LL).
  • Crucial Validation: We want to see a full candle body close past the level — not just a wick sweep!
Takeaway: BOS means the buyers (or sellers) are still strong. Stay with the trend!

Reversal

Market Structure Shift (MSS / CHoCH)

What is an MSS?

An MSS (Market Structure Shift), also known as CHoCH (Change of Character), marks the moment the trend REVERSES and changes direction.

  • Bullish to Bearish: Price in an uptrend fails and breaks BELOW the most recent Higher Low (HL).
  • Bearish to Bullish: Price in a downtrend breaks ABOVE the most recent Lower High (LH).
  • BOS vs MSS: BOS = Continuation | MSS = Reversal.
Crucial Warning: When an MSS occurs, stop buying! The trend has flipped to bearish.
