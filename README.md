# AUPP Trading Club — Market Structure Basics

A clean, beginner-focused presentation slide deck created for the **AUPP Trading Club** at the **American University of Phnom Penh**, teaching the core basics of **Market Structure**:
- Identifying **Uptrend**, **Downtrend**, and **Sideways (Range)**
- Understanding Swing Points: **Higher High (HH)**, **Higher Low (HL)**, **Lower High (LH)**, **Lower Low (LL)**
- **Break of Structure (BOS)**: Trend continuation
- **Market Structure Shift (MSS)**: Trend reversal

Built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, strictly adhering to the official visual identity, typography (Cinzel & Montserrat), and color palette from the Week 1 Fundamentals deck.

---

## 🚀 How to Run & Present
